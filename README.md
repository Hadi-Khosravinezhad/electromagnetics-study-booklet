### 🎯 Motivation & Academic Context
Electromagnetic Theory is historically considered one of the most mathematically demanding courses for undergraduate electrical engineers. This booklet was developed during my two academic semesters serving as a Teaching Assistant (TA). The primary goal was to simplify abstract mathematical formulations (vector operators, contour integrals) and connect them with physical intuition and wave behavior.

---

### 📑 Comprehensive Syllabus & Covered Topics 

1. **Vector Calculus & Foundations:**
   - Orthogonal Coordinate Systems (Cartesian, Cylindrical, Spherical)
   - Vector Differential Operators: Gradient ($\nabla$), Divergence ($\nabla \cdot$), and Curl ($\nabla \times$)
   - Integral Theorems: Divergence (Gauss's) Theorem and Stokes' Theorem

2. **Electrostatic Fields in Media:**
   - Coulomb's Law and Electric Field Intensity ($\mathbf{E}$)
   - Gauss's Law (Integral & Differential forms) and Electric Flux Density ($\mathbf{D}$)
   - Electric Potential ($V$), Poisson's and Laplace's Equations
   - Dielectric Interfaces, Polarization ($\mathbf{P}$), and Boundary Conditions
   - Capacitance calculation and Electrostatic Energy Storage

3. **Magnetostatic Fields:**
   - Biot–Savart Law and Ampère's Circuital Law
   - Magnetic Flux Density ($\mathbf{B}$) and Vector Magnetic Potential ($\mathbf{A}$)
   - Magnetic Materials, Magnetization ($\mathbf{M}$), and Inductance Calculations

4. **Time-Varying Fields & Electrodynamics:**
   - Faraday's Law of Electromagnetic Induction and Lenz's Law
   - Maxwell’s Displacement Current Hypothesis
   - Complete Set of Maxwell's Equations (Time-domain and Phasor representations)
   - Time-varying Boundary Conditions

5. **Introductory Plane Waves:**
   - Helmholtz Wave Equation in Lossless and Dissipative Media
   - Intrinsic Impedance, Phase Velocity, and Skin Depth
   - Power Flow and the Poynting Vector ($\mathbf{S}$)
